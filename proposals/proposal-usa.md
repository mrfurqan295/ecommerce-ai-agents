## 1. The problem

A growing Shopify brand runs on many tools: the store, three ad platforms, email and SMS, a helpdesk and shipping. Each has its own dashboard and its own daily tasks. The result:

- Reporting is manual and arrives late.
- Ad, email and support work competes for the same few people.
- Routine tasks (order status replies, listing updates, creative drafts) crowd out strategy.
- It is hard to see blended performance (MER, cost per customer, repeat rate) in one place.

We set up AI agents to handle the routine work inside limits you set, and to put one clear brief in front of you every morning.

## 2. What we set up

Everything is built inside accounts that **you** own.

| Area | What we set up |
|---|---|
| Store | Shopify with Shopify Payments and Shop Pay; clean product data, policies and guest checkout. |
| Email and SMS | Klaviyo flows: welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase and win-back. SMS only for customers with explicit opt-in. |
| Support | Gorgias AI Agent for order status and returns, with escalation to your team. |
| Shipping | Shopify Shipping or your 3PL. |
| Ads | Meta, Google Shopping and TikTok, with hard spending limits set inside each platform. |
| AI shopping | Product data kept ready for AI shopping channels through Shopify Agentic Storefronts (ChatGPT and Copilot for US buyers). Availability is set by Shopify. |
| Brand Brain | One Airtable base holding your brand voice, products, policies, limits and approvals. Agents use only these facts. |
| Automation | Make scenarios for the daily brief and the approvals workflow. |
| Compliance basics | Real customer reviews only (FTC fake-review rule), SMS consent, email unsubscribe links, sales tax through Shopify. |

## 3. What the AI agents do daily

- Send a morning brief by email: revenue, orders, ad spend, MER/ROAS, cost per customer, repeat rate, chargebacks and open issues.
- Draft product listings, SEO updates, ad creatives and social posts for approval.
- Report on ad performance and propose changes; changes are made only with approval or inside your limits.
- Answer order-status and returns questions through Gorgias and escalate the rest to people.
- Route every request that crosses a threshold to an approvals queue and log the outcome.

Because US daytime is night in Pakistan, routine actions inside your limits run automatically, and urgent approvals go to your team.

## 4. What stays under your control

- **You own every account:** Shopify, Meta, Google, TikTok, Klaviyo, Gorgias, Make and Airtable. We are invited as admin or partner. No shared passwords. You can remove our access at any time.
- **You approve what matters:** new campaigns, budgets above your daily cap, budget changes over 20% in a day, price changes over 10%, refunds above $50, new products, and every public post in the first month. All thresholds are adjustable.
- **Hard spending limits** live inside Meta, Google and TikTok, not only in our system.
- **Staged autonomy.** Agents start at Draft level: they prepare, people approve. They move up only after about two weeks of clean work and a sign-off. Agents that spend money never run fully autonomous.
- **Full audit trail.** Every agent action is logged with before and after values.
- **Honest marketing.** No fake or AI-generated reviews or testimonials, no AI avatars posing as buyers, no health, safety or "best / #1" claims.
