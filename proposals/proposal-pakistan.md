## 1. The problem

Running an online store in Pakistan takes a lot of daily work:

- Every COD order must be confirmed before it ships.
- Refused parcels (RTO) cost you courier charges both ways.
- Customers message on WhatsApp all day, in Urdu, Roman Urdu and English.
- Ads, orders and courier numbers sit in different places, so it is hard to see what a delivered order really costs you.

Most of this work repeats every day. That is the part we hand to AI agents, with you in control.

## 2. What we set up

Everything is built inside accounts that **you** own.

| Area | What we set up |
|---|---|
| Store | Your Shopify store and domain, with clean product pages. |
| Payments | Cash on delivery, plus prepaid through a local payment aggregator (for example bSecure: JazzCash, EasyPaisa, cards). A small discount for prepaid orders, if you agree. |
| Order confirmation | WhatsApp confirmation for every COD order, with Confirm / Cancel. A reminder if there is no reply, and auto-cancel after a time you choose. |
| Couriers | A courier booking app linked to your courier accounts (such as PostEx, TCS, Trax, Leopards, BlueEx). A parcel is booked only after the order is confirmed. |
| RTO control | A list of phone numbers that refused parcels before, and RTO tracked by city, courier and product. |
| Customer support | WhatsApp replies in the customer's language, using approved templates and your own policies. |
| Ads | Meta ads, plus TikTok if it fits your product. Spending limits are set inside the ad platform itself. |
| Brand Brain | One Airtable base with your brand voice, products, policies, limits and approvals. The agents use only these facts. |
| Daily brief | A short report every morning on WhatsApp or email. |

We choose the exact apps with you during setup.

## 3. What the AI agents do every day

- Send you a morning brief: sales, orders, ad spend, confirmation rate, delivered %, RTO % and any problems.
- Confirm COD orders on WhatsApp and tag them in Shopify.
- Send confirmed orders to courier booking and follow the courier status.
- Flag customers who refused parcels before.
- Draft replies to customer questions.
- Draft product listings, ad creatives and social posts for your approval.
- Report on ads and suggest changes. Changes happen only after approval.

## 4. What stays under your control

- **You own every account:** Shopify, Meta, WhatsApp, couriers, payment gateway, Make and Airtable. We are added as admin or partner. No shared passwords. You can remove our access at any time.
- **You approve the important things:** new campaigns, budgets above your daily cap, price changes over 10%, refunds above Rs 3,000, new products, and every public post in the first month. These limits can be changed to suit you.
- **Hard spending limits** are set inside the ad platforms, not only in our system.
- **Agents start slowly.** For the first 2 weeks they only draft; a person checks and sends. They get more freedom only after clean work and sign-off.
- **Everything is logged.** Every agent action is written to a log you can read.
- **Honest marketing only.** No fake reviews, no made-up testimonials, no health or "best / #1" claims.
